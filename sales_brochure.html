ApexTime provides an easy-to-use software solution that covers the daily needs of modern campuses, giving incredible flexibility to staff while keeping your data strictly accurate.

Punch Anywhere Hero Feature

Teachers and staff can log their attendance instantly from any mobile device, laptop, or tablet. Whether on a field trip or working from home, geographic punch-ins are seamless and securely tracked.

All-in-One Staff Portal Hero Feature

Provide employees with a dedicated self-service portal. They can easily review their past attendance status, check monthly balances, request leaves, and stay updated on important campus announcements.

CompOff Management Hero Feature

Automatically track extra hours or holiday work! Our built-in CompOff system banks hours securely so staff can claim extra leaves, while management gets a clear overview before payroll.

Smart Timetables

Create full weekly class schedules without clashes. Assign the right professors to the right subjects, allocate breaks, and efficiently manage daily campus routines.

Direct Biometric Sync

Connect your existing fingerprint or facial recognition machines directly to our software. Devices talk to the cloud in real-time—attendance is marked without a single manual data entry.
